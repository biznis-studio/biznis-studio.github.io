# Design, Branding & Visual Identity

Most small businesses do not need a full rebrand. They need the specific
thing that is currently holding them back: a logo that still works at
favicon size, a colour and type system so everything stops looking like
it came from four different places, or a landing page that does not
undermine the product behind it.

This is design work scoped to what you actually need - and, importantly,
scoped to what can genuinely be delivered well. The list of what is *not*
offered is further down, and it is there because a studio that claims
everything is useless to you.

## What we make

- **Logos and wordmarks** - delivered as SVG, which is true vector: it
  stays sharp at any size and opens in any vector tool for editing.
  Includes flattened PNG exports at the sizes you actually need, and a
  favicon that is still legible at 16 pixels.
- **Visual identity systems** - colour palette, typography, spacing and
  the rules for how they combine, written down so anything made later
  still looks like you. Delivered as a short written guide *and* as a
  working stylesheet, so a developer can implement it without
  interpreting a PDF. Light and dark variants included, since half your
  audience will see the dark one.
- **Web and interface design** - landing pages, product pages, dashboards
  and email templates. Designed directly in real HTML and CSS rather than
  as a picture of a website, so what you approve is the actual thing,
  already responsive and already working. No handoff gap where the build
  turns out different from the mockup.
- **Marketing graphics** - social images, banners, covers, ad creatives
  and product mockups, exported at the exact dimensions each platform
  wants.
- **Presentation and document design** - pitch decks, one-pagers,
  proposals and reports, delivered as PDF or PowerPoint.
- **Icon sets** - consistent SVG icon sets drawn to match your identity.

## What we do not offer

This is the honest part, and it is what makes the list above trustworthy:

- **Hand illustration, character art and bespoke drawing.** Geometric and
  abstract vector work, yes. A drawn mascot or editorial illustration,
  no - hire an illustrator, they will do it far better.
- **Photography.** We source properly licensed photography and can direct
  what is needed, but we do not shoot it.
- **Print production.** This is digital-first. Screen, web and export
  formats are covered; CMYK separations, bleed setup and prepress for a
  commercial printer are not.
- **Layered PSD or Figma source files.** Deliverables are SVG, CSS, HTML
  and PDF - genuinely editable, open formats. If your team specifically
  needs a Figma library to work from, we are not the right fit.

## Who this is for

- **Businesses whose materials do not match** - a logo from one place, a
  deck from a template, social graphics made in a hurry. The fix is
  usually a small set of rules, not a pile of new assets.
- **Anyone launching something** where first impressions carry weight and
  there is no in-house designer.
- **Clients of the other services here** - if we are building your site,
  your digital product or your automation, having it designed to your
  brand rather than a default is the obvious pairing.

## How it works

1. **Tell us what is not working.** A specific symptom beats a general
   brief - "our deck loses people on slide three" points somewhere real.
2. **Written proposal.** Exactly what you get, in which formats, how many
   revision rounds, fixed price, timeline. Agreed before anything starts.
3. **Concepts applied to your real content**, not polished mockups that
   hide the awkward cases. Two revision rounds included as standard.
4. **You own everything.** Full commercial rights, source files included,
   no licensing terms and no ongoing fees.

## What it costs

Scope decides it, so an honest figure needs a conversation first. A logo
with basic usage rules sits at the lower end; a full identity system with
templates and a designed site is a larger piece of work.

You get the exact number in the proposal before committing. If the budget
does not match the scope, we will say so and suggest what to cut - one
piece done properly beats a full package done thinly.
