# Tailored Digital Product

Everything else on this site was built from a demand signal we found -
which means it was built for a problem lots of people share, not
necessarily the one you have. This is the opposite: you describe the
problem, and we build the digital product for it.

## What this actually is

You bring a brief. We build a finished, ready-to-use digital product
around it - the same kinds of things you can already see on this site,
made specifically for your situation, your wording, and your industry:

- **Script and template kits** - the exact wording for the conversations
  your role keeps repeating, in your voice rather than a generic one.
- **Checklists and SOPs** - a repeatable process written down properly,
  so it survives handover, holidays, and new hires.
- **Calculators and interactive tools** - a browser tool that answers one
  specific question for your clients or your team, branded as yours.
- **Trackers and spreadsheet systems** - the CSV/spreadsheet layer that
  makes a process actually stick instead of living in someone's inbox.
- **Lead magnets** - a genuinely useful free resource your audience wants
  to trade an email address for.

## Who this is for

- **Consultants and agencies** who want a branded resource to give
  clients, without spending three weekends writing it.
- **Small teams** with a process that works but exists only in one
  person's head, and becomes a problem the moment they are away.
- **Course creators and newsletter writers** who need a companion
  workbook, template pack, or tool for an existing audience.
- **Anyone** who has repeatedly thought "there should be a template for
  this" and found that there genuinely isn't.

## Who this isn't for

- You need a full software product, a web app with user accounts, or an
  ongoing SaaS - that is a development project, not a digital product.
  (We do build custom sites and chatbots - that is a separate service.)
- You want content produced at volume for SEO purposes. We build one
  thing properly rather than fifty things quickly.
- You need it in under a few days. Good briefs need a conversation first.

## How it works

1. **Tell us the problem.** Not the product you think you want - the
   situation that keeps costing you time or money. A few paragraphs is
   plenty to start.
2. **We scope it, in writing.** You get back a concrete proposal: what
   the deliverable is, what format it takes, what it will and will not
   cover, the price, and the timeline. Fixed price, agreed before any
   work starts - no hourly billing, no surprise invoice.
3. **We build it.** You get a draft to react to, not a finished thing
   presented as final. One full revision round is included as standard.
4. **You own it outright.** Full commercial rights to the finished
   files - sell it, give it away, put your name on it, edit it forever.
   We do not resell your product to anyone else, and we do not add it to
   this site.

## What it costs

Pricing depends entirely on scope, so an honest number needs the brief
first. As a rough orientation: a focused script or template kit of the
size you see on this site sits in the low hundreds; a multi-part system
with a custom interactive tool costs meaningfully more.

You get the exact figure in the proposal, before committing to anything.
If the scope does not justify the cost, we will say so - a template pack
you could assemble yourself in an afternoon is not a good use of your
money.

## Getting started

Send a short description of the problem using the form below. Useful
things to include: who the product is for, what they should be able to
do after using it, and any deadline you are working to. If you already
have half-finished notes or an existing document, mention that too -
starting from real material is usually faster and better than starting
from a blank page.
