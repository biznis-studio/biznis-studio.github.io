# Custom Website Design & Development

A website built specifically for what your business actually needs -
not a generic template with your logo dropped in. This page itself, and
every product on this site, was built the same way: fast, clean,
functional, and without unnecessary bloat.

## What's included

- A site designed around your actual content and goals, not a
  one-size-fits-all theme
- Clean, fast-loading pages - no unnecessary plugins or page-builder bloat
  slowing things down
- Mobile-friendly by default, since most visitors will be on a phone
- Basic on-page SEO (proper titles, descriptions, structured content) so
  search engines can actually understand your site
- Deployed and working - not just a design file handed over at the end

## What this does not cover

Stated plainly, because a studio that claims everything is useless to you:

- **Web applications with user accounts, logins and a database** behind
  them. Marketing sites, product sites, landing pages and interactive
  browser tools, yes. A SaaS product, no.
- **Online shops taking card payments directly.** We integrate an
  existing checkout - Gumroad, Stripe Payment Links, Lemon Squeezy - but
  the merchant account is yours, in your name, and the payment provider
  handles the money and the tax.
- **Ongoing content updates as a retainer.** You get a site you can edit,
  and documentation for how.

## Who this is for

Small businesses, freelancers, and solo founders who need a real,
working website - not a business that already has a large in-house
design team, and not a project that needs a massive, custom-built web
application from scratch.

## How it works

- Get in touch describing what you need and roughly what it's for
- A short back-and-forth to nail down scope, pages, and any specific
  functionality
- A quote based on the actual scope - no fixed public price list, since a
  five-page brochure site and a site with a booking system are very
  different amounts of work
- Build, review, adjust, launch
