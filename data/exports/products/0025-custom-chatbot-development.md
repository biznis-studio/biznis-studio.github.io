# Custom Chatbot Development

A chatbot built for one specific job - answering your customers'
questions, qualifying leads, or guiding people to the right page - not a
generic bolt-on widget that gives vague, unhelpful answers.

## What this can cover

- A chatbot trained on your actual business information (your products,
  your FAQ, your policies) so answers are accurate, not generic
- Embedded directly into your existing website or a new one
- Scoped to what it should and shouldn't answer, so it doesn't
  confidently make things up about your business
- Handoff to a real contact method (email, form) for anything it
  genuinely can't help with

## Who this is for

Businesses that get the same handful of questions repeatedly (hours,
pricing structure, how something works, where to find something) and
want those answered instantly, at any hour, without a generic script
that frustrates people.

## How it works

- Get in touch with what you'd want the chatbot to actually handle
- A short scoping conversation - what it should know, what it should
  hand off to a human, where it should live
- A quote based on that scope - a simple FAQ-answering bot and a bot
  that needs to look up live data are very different amounts of work
- Build, test with real questions, adjust, launch

One practical point stated up front: a chatbot needs access to a language
model, which is a paid third-party service billed per use. That account
stays in your name and you pay for it directly - we never put your usage
on our billing, and you are never locked into us to keep it running.

A chatbot is only as good as its scope - it works best for well-defined,
repeatable questions, not as a stand-in for a person handling every
possible situation. Any proposal will be honest about what's realistic
for your specific case.
